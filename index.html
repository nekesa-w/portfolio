WHO AM I?

Hello, I am Nicole Wasike. Front-end Developer, UI/UX Designer and Artist based in Nairobi, Kenya. I like building things that people enjoy using. I like clean code, but I also messy sketches. I like breaking things, fixing them, and learning more in the process.

MOCKUP: PAMO

Pamo is a food waste donation app mockup that connects individuals who have excess food with those in need. Its interface focuses on easy food listing, real-time availability tracking, and location-based matching, using a calm teal and green color palette.

MOCKUP: AFYAZONE

Afyazone is a modern fitness app mockup focusing on gym services. The design features a bold black background contrasted with vibrant lime green accents for buttons, highlights, and icons. The interface conveys a dynamic, high-energy gym experience while maintaining a modern, minimalist aesthetic.

MOCKUP: NUFASA

Nufasa Tours and Travels is a travel website mockup designed to showcase destinations, tours, and travel experiences. The design emphasizes rich colors like maroon and gold, and high-quality photography.

MOCKUP: NASSCONSULT

NassConsult Real Estate is a luxury real estate mockup featuring a sophisticated palette of gold and navy blue. The design emphasizes prestige and refinement, showcasing properties with a polished, high-end aesthetic that conveys exclusivity.
